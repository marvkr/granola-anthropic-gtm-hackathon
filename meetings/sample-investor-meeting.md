# Seed pitch: Lumen x Northbeam Ventures

## Summary

Alex and Sam pitched Lumen, an AI agent that writes and sends personalized
follow-ups after sales calls. Priya was positive on the team and the problem,
but wants more proof that the demand isn't just a few design partners and
clearer unit economics before bringing it to Monday's partner meeting.
Daniel will run diligence on the pipeline numbers.

## What resonated

- **Founder-market fit.** Alex ran sales at a Series B company and lived the problem.
- **Speed to value.** The live demo went from transcript to sent email in under
  60 seconds, and Priya called it "the best part of the pitch."
- **Design partner quotes** on slide 6 landed well.

## Concerns raised

- **Market size feels hand-wavy.** The TAM slide uses a top-down "all sales
  reps" number. Priya wants a bottom-up calculation: number of target
  companies × reps per company × price.
- **Competition.** Asked how Lumen is different from Gong and Salesforce's
  built-in AI follow-ups. The current competition slide is a 2×2 that
  "every deck has" and doesn't answer the question.
- **Retention unknown.** Only 4 months of usage data. She asked for weekly
  active usage per seat, not just logo count.
- **Pricing.** Unclear whether pricing is per seat or usage-based. The deck
  says both on different slides.

## Requests for the follow-up

1. Replace the TAM slide with a bottom-up market sizing.
2. Add a slide on why Lumen wins against Gong / Salesforce Einstein, with
   concrete product differences, not a 2×2.
3. Add a retention / engagement chart: weekly active seats by cohort.
4. Make pricing consistent across the deck, and show one example contract.
5. Move the live demo (or a GIF of it) earlier, ideally slide 3.
6. Add a "use of funds" slide: how the $3M breaks down and which milestones
   it hits in 18 months.
